# Comparison Study of Psych Emergency/Hospitalization Rates Between Pre and Post Admission of Patients in Partial Hospitalization and Intensive Outpatient Psychiatric (IOP) clinic Shows Benefits

- Authors: Jordan Pelot, MA; Maurice Fried, PhD; Laura Cocas, PhD; Chanic Kim, BS; Craig Metz, MA
- AHS Psych; 15400 Foothill Blvd, San Leandro, CA 94578
- Corr. Author: Maurice Fried (mfried@alamedahealthsystem.org)

AIM: To measure the difference between rates of psych emergency and inpatient psychiatric facility contacts pre and post admission of SPMI patients to the day treatment program at Outpatient Behavioral Health Services at Fairmont Hospital Campus of Alameda Health System.

## Overview

- **Question/Purpose:** To what degree does IOP contact reduce hospitalization rates among SMI?
- **Context:** Fairmont IOP offers outpatient treatment in the form of group therapy, social services, etc. to patients with SMI
- **Status:** Complete (To be published)

## Data

- **Source:** Patients attending IOP previously hospitalized --> includes psychiatric hospitals, residential psychiatric rehabilitation facilities (Villa Fairmont, Woodrow House, etc), and psychiatric jail (Santa Rita)
- **Size:** e.g. N=82 IOP Patients
- **Privacy note:** No personal patient information included

## Key Findings

- Significantly lower rate of hospitalization and Psychiatric Emergency Services (PES) visits after IOP contact
    - Mann-Whitney non-parametric two-tailed test; p=<0.0001; N=2
- 167 days per patient (14,331 total days) saved due to lower rate of hospitalization --> $28,872,000 estimated total savings ($500/PES visit & $2,000 per inpatient day)
